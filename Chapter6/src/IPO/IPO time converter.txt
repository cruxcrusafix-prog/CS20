	The user will input in the program how many seconds they would like to add (until positive integer limit)
	They will split the amount of seconds into 3 areas, days, hours, and minutes. 
	if there are >86400 seconds, they will be put in the day category, 3600 for hour, and 60 for minute. Anything that is 59 seconds or below will not be counted
	The program will display how many days, hours, and/or minutes they have inputted through seconds. 
	If there is only 1 of a category, said category will have the "S" at the end removed to singularize the word.
