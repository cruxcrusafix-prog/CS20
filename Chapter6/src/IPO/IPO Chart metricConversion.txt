	The user will be asked to pick their desired number to convert from one metric/imperial unit to another metric/imperial unit. Itwill then pick a number from 1-8 on the conversion they’d like.
	(Example if the user picked option 2, feet to centimetres) The user wanted to convert 3 feet into centimetres. The program uses the switch statement to look through the 8 options, 
	and it will look for case 2. Case 2 multiplies the chosen number to convert by 30.48 times, and the program will multiply 3 feet 30.48 so it’ll show the length in centimetres. 
	It will also shorten decimal counts so as to not make the output length 10 decimals long.
	
	The program will output your metric and what it converts to. So for 3 feet to centimetres, it’ll output “3.00 feet will give you 91.440 centimetres.”
